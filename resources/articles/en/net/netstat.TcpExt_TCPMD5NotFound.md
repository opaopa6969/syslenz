# TcpExt_TCPMD5NotFound

[日本語版](../../ja/net/netstat.TcpExt_TCPMD5NotFound.md)

Part of the **[TcpExt group](TcpExt.md)** — extended TCP statistics from `/proc/net/netstat`.

`TcpExt_TCPMD5NotFound` — TCP segments with expected but missing MD5 signature.

**Source:** `/proc/net/netstat`  
**Unit:** cumulative count
