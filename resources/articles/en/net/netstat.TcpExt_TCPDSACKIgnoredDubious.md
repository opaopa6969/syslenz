# TcpExt_TCPDSACKIgnoredDubious

[日本語版](../../ja/net/netstat.TcpExt_TCPDSACKIgnoredDubious.md)

---

Part of the **[TcpExt group](TcpExt.md)** — extended TCP statistics from `/proc/net/netstat`.

`TcpExt_TCPDSACKIgnoredDubious` — DSACK blocks ignored as dubious (questionable sender behavior).

**Source:** `/proc/net/netstat`  
**Unit:** cumulative count
