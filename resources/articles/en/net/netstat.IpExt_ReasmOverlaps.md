# IpExt_ReasmOverlaps

Part of the **[IpExt group](IpExt.md)** — extended IP statistics from `/proc/net/netstat`.

`IpExt_ReasmOverlaps` — IP reassembly overlapping fragments (possible attack or buggy sender).

**Source:** `/proc/net/netstat`  
**Unit:** cumulative count
