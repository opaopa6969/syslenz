# TcpExt_RcvPruned

[日本語版](../../ja/net/netstat.TcpExt_RcvPruned.md)

Part of the **[TcpExt group](TcpExt.md)** — extended TCP statistics from `/proc/net/netstat`.

`TcpExt_RcvPruned` — Packets pruned from receive queue due to memory pressure.

**Source:** `/proc/net/netstat`  
**Unit:** cumulative count
