# TcpExt_TCPDSACKOldSent

[日本語版](../../ja/net/netstat.TcpExt_TCPDSACKOldSent.md)

Part of the **[TcpExt group](TcpExt.md)** — extended TCP statistics from `/proc/net/netstat`.

`TcpExt_TCPDSACKOldSent` — DSACK sent for old (already received) data.

**Source:** `/proc/net/netstat`  
**Unit:** cumulative count
