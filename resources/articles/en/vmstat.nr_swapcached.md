# nr_swapcached

[日本語版](../ja/vmstat.nr_swapcached.md)

---

`nr_swapcached` — Swap pages also present in RAM (avoids re-reading from swap on reuse).

**Source:** `/proc/vmstat`  
**Unit:** cumulative count (monotonically increasing since boot)
