# oom_kill

[日本語版](../ja/vmstat.oom_kill.md)

---

`oom_kill` — OOM killer invocations — processes killed due to memory exhaustion.

**Source:** `/proc/vmstat`  
**Unit:** cumulative count (monotonically increasing since boot)
