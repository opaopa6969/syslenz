# pgpgout

[日本語版](../ja/vmstat.pgpgout.md)

---

`pgpgout` — Pages paged out to disk (in 1KB units).

**Source:** `/proc/vmstat`  
**Unit:** cumulative count (monotonically increasing since boot)
