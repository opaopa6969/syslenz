Part of the **[vmstat.numa](../vmstat.numa.md)** family — see the group article for full context, tuning, and real episodes.

`numa_miss` — NUMA allocations that landed on the wrong node (remote memory).

**Source:** `/proc/vmstat`  
**Unit:** cumulative count (monotonically increasing since boot)
