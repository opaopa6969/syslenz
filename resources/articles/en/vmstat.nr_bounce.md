# nr_bounce

[日本語版](../ja/vmstat.nr_bounce.md)

---

`nr_bounce` — Bounce buffer pages (used when DMA can't access high memory).

**Source:** `/proc/vmstat`  
**Unit:** cumulative count (monotonically increasing since boot)
