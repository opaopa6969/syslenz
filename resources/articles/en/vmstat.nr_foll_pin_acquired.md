# nr_foll_pin_acquired

[日本語版](../ja/vmstat.nr_foll_pin_acquired.md)

---

`nr_foll_pin_acquired` — GUP pin-fast page references acquired (RDMA, DMA pinning).

**Source:** `/proc/vmstat`  
**Unit:** cumulative count (monotonically increasing since boot)
