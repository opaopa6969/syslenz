# pgpgin

[日本語版](../ja/vmstat.pgpgin.md)

---

`pgpgin` — Pages paged in from disk (in 1KB units, despite the name).

**Source:** `/proc/vmstat`  
**Unit:** cumulative count (monotonically increasing since boot)
