Part of the **[vmstat.pgalloc](../vmstat.pgalloc.md)** family — see the group article for full context, tuning, and real episodes.

`pgskip_dma32` — DMA32 zone skipped during reclaim scan (nothing to reclaim).

**Source:** `/proc/vmstat`  
**Unit:** cumulative count (monotonically increasing since boot)
