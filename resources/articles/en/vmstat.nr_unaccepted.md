# nr_unaccepted

[日本語版](../ja/vmstat.nr_unaccepted.md)

---

`nr_unaccepted` — Unaccepted memory pages (confidential VMs, not yet accepted by guest).

**Source:** `/proc/vmstat`  
**Unit:** cumulative count (monotonically increasing since boot)
