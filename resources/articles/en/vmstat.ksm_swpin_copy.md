# ksm_swpin_copy

[日本語版](../ja/vmstat.ksm_swpin_copy.md)

---

`ksm_swpin_copy` — KSM pages copied on swap-in (can't swap in shared, need private copy).

**Source:** `/proc/vmstat`  
**Unit:** cumulative count (monotonically increasing since boot)
