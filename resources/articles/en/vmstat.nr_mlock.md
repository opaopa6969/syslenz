# nr_mlock

[日本語版](../ja/vmstat.nr_mlock.md)

---

`nr_mlock` — Pages locked with mlock() — cannot be swapped or evicted.

**Source:** `/proc/vmstat`  
**Unit:** cumulative count (monotonically increasing since boot)
